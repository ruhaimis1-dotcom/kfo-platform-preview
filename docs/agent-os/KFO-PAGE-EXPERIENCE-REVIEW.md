# KFO page experience preview — review only

Status: unapproved visual concept for Saud's review. Route: `/review` on an isolated preview branch.

The switcher shows three illustrative screens: public home, company training dashboard, and company employee portal. The existing KFO logo, IBM Plex Sans Arabic/Inter weight set, locked colors, RTL, approved public hero composition and approved tenant/role architecture are preserved. The desktop hero is sourced from the approved 20 Sep checkpoint; the mobile presentation reuses its approved image and typesets the same message for legibility.

The home concept adds a role explanation, three-step company journey and an actual dashboard sample under the approved hero. The company screen presents a task-focused ordering using the same 96 demo assignments and 38 completed items as D0; it links to the interactive D0 preview for the four state variants. The employee screen is a proposed company learner portal with resume learning, assigned courses, company library, certificates and requests. It is distinct from the company administrative workspace.

All sample people, dates, numbers and statuses are fictional. Links in these concept screens only switch previews or visit the existing static D0/login routes. No backend, entitlement, learning persistence, tenant routing or authorization was added. In particular, the public home and individual learner approved previews have not been superseded. Review the concept on desktop and an actual phone before any design decision; keep PRs as drafts and do not merge/release through this preview.
